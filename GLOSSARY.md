# The Prophets and Apostles

Cited search over the words of Church leaders and over the standard works, so every answer quotes its source exactly.

## Language

### Talks

**Talk**:
One complete piece by one or more speakers: a General Conference talk, a devotional or broadcast, a magazine article, or a BYU speech.
_Avoid_: Article, speech, document (except when naming that specific kind)

**Speaker**:
A Church leader a talk is credited to, whether it was spoken or written.
_Avoid_: Author, leader

**Joint talk**:
A talk credited to more than one speaker, shown with everyone's names and never pinned on one of them.
_Avoid_: Co-authored talk, panel

**Source**:
Where a talk was given or published: General Conference, BYU Speeches, Church broadcast, Liahona, Ensign, New Era, or For the Strength of Youth.
_Avoid_: Publication, venue, type

**Talk ID**:
A talk's lasting name, taken from its source link, such as `general-conference/2025/04/47oaks` or `byu/ronald-a-rasband/hurrah-for-israel`.
_Avoid_: URL, slug, key

**Paragraph**:
One block of a talk's own text, numbered in reading order from 1 (written ¶12). Footnotes and captions are not paragraphs.
_Avoid_: Block, section

**Footnote**:
A numbered note a talk attaches to one of its paragraphs by a marker, giving a scripture reference or the source of a quotation.
_Avoid_: Note, endnote, reference (a reference names verses)

**Passage**:
A paragraph returned as a search result, with its talk, speaker, date and link.
_Avoid_: Hit, snippet, excerpt

### Scriptures

**Standard works**:
The five volumes of scripture: Old Testament, New Testament, Book of Mormon, Doctrine and Covenants, and Pearl of Great Price.
_Avoid_: Scriptures (as a proper name), canon

**Verse**:
One numbered unit of a chapter, named by its reference, such as Moroni 10:4.
_Avoid_: Passage (that word belongs to talks)

**Reference**:
The written name of one or more verses: a book, a chapter, and optionally a verse or range, such as Moroni 10:4-5 or Alma 32.
_Avoid_: Citation (reserved for a talk pointing at a verse)

**Citation**:
A paragraph of a talk pointing at one or more verses through a link or a written reference, in its text or its footnote. Every verse in a cited range or list is cited; a verse quoted without a reference is not.
_Avoid_: Quote, mention

**Citation count**:
The number of distinct talks that cite a verse; a talk citing it many times counts once.
_Avoid_: Popularity, frequency, hits

**Chapter citation**:
A citation of a whole chapter with no verses named, such as John 1. It does not count as citing any single verse.
_Avoid_: Citation (when meaning a whole chapter)
